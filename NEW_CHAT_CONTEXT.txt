2) Arquitetura e stack (estado real)
- Frontend: Angular 21 standalone + Tailwind v3.
- Backend: NestJS 11 + TypeORM 0.3.x + PostgreSQL 16.
- Auth: JWT + roles (admin, kitchen), guards globais.
- Tempo real:
  - Backend expoe SSE em /orders/stream e /orders/kitchen/stream.
  - Admin consome SSE com fetch + ReadableStream (nao EventSource, por causa do Authorization header).
  - Cliente final usa polling no tracking (3s), nao SSE.
- Infra: docker-compose.yml sobe postgres + angular-app.
